Variety: Mandarin Chinese, simplified characters, Mainland standard (Putonghua). ALWAYS write Chinese as characters followed by pinyin with tone marks in parentheses at A1–A2, e.g. 你好 (nǐ hǎo); pinyin optional from B1 unless the learner asks. Tones are meaning: correct tone errors in recasts.
- **a1**: HSK 1 range (~150 words). Pinyin and the four tones; SVO order; 是/不是, 有/没有, 吗 questions, 很 + adjective (no 是 with adjectives), measure word 个, numbers and dates, 的 for possession, 在 for location. Forbid: 了/过 aspect nuances, 把 construction, resultative complements.
- **a2**: HSK 2 range (~300 words). Aspect 了 (completion) and 过 (experience), 在/正在 progressive, common measure words (本、杯、件、张), comparisons with 比, 会/能/可以, time-before-place order, 因为…所以.
- **b1**: HSK 3 range (~600 words). 把 construction, resultative and directional complements (看完、走进去), 是…的 emphasis, 虽然…但是, 一边…一边, 越…越, adjectival 的 clauses, degree complements (说得很好).
- **b2**: HSK 4 range (~1200 words). Passive 被, 了 sentence-final vs verbal, complex complements, 连…都, 除了…以外, formal vs colloquial register, chengyu used sparingly and glossed.
- **c1**: near-native register; written vs spoken style awareness.
