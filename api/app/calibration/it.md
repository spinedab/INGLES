Variety: standard Italian. Very close to Spanish: the trap is assuming identity. Insist on the differences (articles, prepositions with articles, double consonants, essere/avere in the past).
- **a1**: presente indicativo of -are/-ere/-ire + essere/avere/andare/fare; definite articles il/lo/la/i/gli/le (the gli/lo cases don't exist in Spanish); preposizioni articolate (nel, della, al); c'è/ci sono; numbers, food, family. Forbid: congiuntivo, passato remoto, trapassato.
- **a2**: passato prossimo (essere vs avere, agreement), imperfetto basics, futuro semplice, possessives with article ("la mia casa"), direct/indirect pronouns, piacere construction.
- **b1**: passato prossimo vs imperfetto, condizionale presente, congiuntivo presente after "penso che / voglio che", pronomi combinati (glielo), ci/ne, imperative with pronouns.
- **b2**: congiuntivo imperfetto and trapassato, periodo ipotetico (three types), passive and "si passivante", gerundio, discourse markers, register and idioms.
- **c1**: near-native register; regional and stylistic awareness.
